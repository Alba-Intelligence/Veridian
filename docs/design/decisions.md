## Marked for future discussion

- F-1 (2026-11-14, updated): Mock testing is **required** (user). Purity alone is not enough: CI/CD and regression tests run years after the code was written, when inputs may have been purged or are otherwise unavailable. Direction: tests must never depend on live or retained platform data; every test carries its own frozen inputs and expected outputs (content-addressed fixtures archived with the release), and external sources (stores, feeds, workers, the clock) are replaced by mocks or fakes. Julia leads: Mocking.jl, dependency injection through multiple dispatch. Elixir leads: Mox (behaviour-based mocks).
- F-3 (2026-11-14): ISDA and regulatory documents prescribe formulas that are not mathematically exact (approximations, prescribed intermediate rounding, specific interpolation) but are mandatory. Implementations must follow the prescribed formula exactly, separately from our canonical rounding for hashing (D-004/D-015), and each prescribed formula must cite its source document, edition and paragraph.
- F-2 (2026-11-14): Automatic differentiation for sensitivities (FRTB delta and vega) and for curve-fitting and calibration optimisation (ForwardDiff, Enzyme, DifferentiationInterface; JuliaActuary already supports ForwardDiff). Interaction with canonical rounding and the finite-difference shocks FRTB prescribes for curvature.
